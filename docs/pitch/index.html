Why it can be trusted

Three properties no spreadsheet has

Exit-block is emergent, not assumed
The model contains the hospital around the ED. When wards fill, admissions back up and corridor care appears - because the bed stock filled, not because a consultant typed a fudge factor. Turn the feedback off and the naive model returns.
It proves its own faithfulness
Every simulated patient journey is checked against the intended pathway (process-map conformance). A clean run passes at 100%; corrupted runs are caught. Clinical teams see the validation, not a promise.
Honest uncertainty, honest money
Every figure is a Monte-Carlo range, every pound comes from a trust-editable cost book with the assumptions printed on the page. Synthetic placeholders are flagged until your data replaces them.
Real ED physics
Ambulance handover bays that block when the front door crowds. Patients who walk out when the wait beats their patience. Cubicles that close when the nursing rota thins. Porters who move admissions. Sickest first, always.

Decision support

Ask it the questions you actually have

"Would we cope with a bad flu season?"
Named stress tests - flu, norovirus ward closures, a strike day, heatwave - run against your ED exactly as configured. Then the inverse: the cheapest staffing additions that hold the 4-hour target, or an honest "staffing alone will not hold it".
"What should we change first?"
The recommendation engine searches your own weak points - rota gaps, pinch resources, opening hours - and only recommends changes whose improvement clears a statistical confidence bar. It would rather say nothing than recommend noise.
"What does this winter look like?"
Upload your daily history: the forecast learns your trend, weekday shape, seasonality and bank-holiday spikes - and shows its own backtested accuracy before you rely on it.
"Today is running hot - how bad by midnight?"
Type this morning's arrivals. It projects the day from your own curve, flags early escalation, and ranks the no-cost actions that help most tonight.
