Where waits build up

Measured as waiting patient-hours per day across the whole run (the number of people waiting at each stage, added up over time). A busy area is not, by itself, a bottleneck; this measures waiting.

Patient flow

Five summary groups, left to right. This is a display grouping, not a compulsory sequence: not everyone needs diagnostics, assessment by RAT or admission; patients can go home early, go straight to SDEC, or return from diagnostics to a decision. Select a group to open its detail underneath.
