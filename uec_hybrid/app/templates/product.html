Ask a what-if

Type a change in plain English and the model will run it against your department — e.g. “add 2 doctors”, “add 3 nurses at 1pm”, “remove a doctor overnight”, “10% more attendances”, “40 more ward beds”, “divert 20% of minors” — or a goal like “what would it take to reach 90% in 4 hours?”. Time windows like “from 8pm to 8am” apply the change to just those hours.

Decision log

Record what the team decided and why — each entry is stamped with the model state it was decided against, and entries can never be edited or deleted (audit-safe).

Start from your trust

Skip the manual set-up: load your trust's published figures, or import a filled-in spreadsheet. Either way the fields below fill in automatically — you can then fine-tune and re-run.

Download the template, fill in the tabs (areas, beds, rota, optional arrivals), then upload it here — no coding needed.

Set up your emergency department

Describe your department; the control tower then models how it runs. Areas you switch off have their patients re-routed safely.

Set each area's opening hours (24-hour clock). Outside those hours its patients are looked after on the main pathway, so same-day areas aren't modelled as running overnight. Leave as 0 to 24 for round-the-clock.
